tooth

Predict tooth shape

It's a mobile application designed for both patients and dentists. I contributed to its development by collecting data, training, managing the training process, and communicating with investors. It features two AI models: the first uses the current tooth shape as input and generates the best possible post-treatment tooth shape; the second uses the current tooth shape and desired treatment plan as input, and it predicts the post-treatment tooth shape.
